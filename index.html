BEHIND THE WEAVE

Made to hold a story.
Never get in its way.

LOOM borrows from the quiet craft of bookbinding and woven cloth. The timeline becomes a thread; the playhead, a shuttle. A solid paper-like control surface keeps the essentials readable, whatever the film brings.

Familiar controls. Generous touch targets. Visible keyboard focus. Less motion when you ask for it. Because thoughtful design should be felt by everyone.
